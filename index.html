About THWP

THWP isn't your average rock band. Part puppet show, part gig, all mayhem.

Born from the unholy union of felt and fuzz pedals, THWP brings a unique blend of rock music and puppet performance that you won't find anywhere else. The band delivers original tracks that range from blistering rock anthems to surprisingly tender ballads — all performed by puppets with more attitude than most humans.

With their debut EP Whiskey Tea (2017), the full-length Puppetual Motion (2022), and new albums on the way, THWP has carved out a niche that's entirely their own. Equal parts absurd and genuinely great music, this is a band that has to be seen to be believed.
