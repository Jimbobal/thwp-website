About

Not your average
rock band.

THWP brings a unique blend of original rock music that ranges from blistering anthems to surprisingly tender ballads. With raw energy, tight musicianship, and songs that stick with you long after the gig.

From debut EP Whiskey Tea (2017) through the full-length Puppetual Motion (2022) to two new albums — The Wee Hours and The Abandoned Gauntlet (2026), out now on all platforms — THWP has carved out a niche that's entirely their own.
